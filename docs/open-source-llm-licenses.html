Permissive OSS (Apache 2.0 / MIT)

Allows commercial hosting, fine-tuning, distillation, and redistribution with copyright notice. Apache 2.0 adds an explicit patent grant.

Community Commercial

Llama Community, Gemma, and Modified MIT allow commercial products for most organizations, while adding clauses such as Meta's 700M monthly active user threshold or restrictions on training competing base models.

Research / Non-Commercial

Mistral Research License (MRL) and CC-BY-NC-4.0 allow free evaluation and academic work, while commercial production requires a separate enterprise agreement.
