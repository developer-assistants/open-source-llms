Hybrid thinking vs always-on reasoning

Models such as Qwen3, DeepSeek-V3.1, and GLM-4.5 let you toggle thinking mode per request, so simple formatting prompts stay fast while hard debugging prompts use full chain of thought. Dedicated reasoning models like DeepSeek-R1 always emit reasoning traces.
