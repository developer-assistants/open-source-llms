Category winners at a glance

Frontier reasoning cluster

DeepSeek-R1 and DeepSeek-V3.1 deliver top math, logic, and code generation under the MIT license when you have multi-GPU H100 or B200 capacity.

Best single 24 GB GPU model

Qwen3-32B handles general chat, multilingual work, and coding on one RTX 3090 or RTX 4090 at 4-bit quantization under Apache 2.0.

Best local coding agent

Devstral Small (24B) and Qwen2.5-Coder-32B pair low latency with multi-file editing and fill-in-the-middle completion.

Best long-context RAG

Llama 4 Scout stretches context to 10M tokens on a single quantized 80 GB GPU, while Llama 4 Maverick handles 1M tokens with multimodal inputs.
